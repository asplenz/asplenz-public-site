Reference content for /oem. Actual page is a custom React layout ; this markdown is the source of the wording.

## Hero

- Eyebrow : OEM · PARTNERS
- Heading : Build your compliance platform on Knowledge
- Sub : For technology providers reselling to regulated institutions. Ship your platform without becoming a compliance backend company.

## Why partner with Knowledge

- Compliance backbone shipped : runtime + audit + human workflow + operator dashboard as one product
- Own your brand : your logo, product name, colours in the surfaces your client institutions see
- Own your customers : your contracts, your commercial relationship, your billing

## What OEM includes

Everything Knowledge Enterprise ships, plus the multi-institution reselling layer :
- White-label UX with your branding
- OEM redistribution rights defined by contract
- Multi-institution management
- Multi-tenant operator dashboard
- Per-tenant health and activity monitoring
- Cross-tenant LLM consumption and cost dashboards
- Centralised platform administration
- Partner onboarding and enablement
- Deployment and environment management
- Product updates included
- Support model calibrated to your redistribution obligations

## The confidentiality wall

As platform vendor, you see :
- Tenant list, IDs and provisioning status
- Consumption metrics per tenant
- FinOps dashboards and billing per tenant
- Per-tenant health and activity
- Org-scoped API keys
- Branding configuration

You never see :
- Your client institutions' rules and policies
- Their consultations, verdicts and precedence traces
- Their overrides and justifications
- Their approval decisions and comments
- Their audit trail contents
- Their compliance officers' individual actions

Enforced at the API layer : org-scoped API keys return 403 on any tenant-content route. Structural, not a role setting.

## How it works

1. Signature : commercial terms agreed (per-tenant, distribution scope, support level, exclusivity if any)
2. Provisioning : your Organisation is provisioned, you receive org-scoped API keys, admin dashboard, billing view
3. Branding : you configure logo, product name, colour, email domain
4. Onboarding : you add client institutions as tenants, each isolated, you bill them, we bill you
