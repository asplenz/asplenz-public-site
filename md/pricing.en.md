Knowledge is currently available through our Founding Design Partner Program. We start with one production-relevant decision, establish measurable success criteria, and run Knowledge alongside your existing process before moving to production.

## Design Partner

**Fixed-scope engagement**

One production-relevant decision. Typically eight weeks. Historical replay or shadow deployment. Success criteria agreed before kickoff.

Pricing is scoped upfront based on the decision, the integration requirements and the implementation support required.

**[Learn about the Founding Design Partner Program](/design-partners)**

## Production

**Annual platform agreement**

Production pricing reflects the scale and deployment requirements of your Knowledge environment — not how many rules you formalize.

Founding design partners receive preferential first-year production terms.
